Privacy Policy

Green Hydroponic Light for Android (co.tjohns.tim.greenhydroponiclight)
Effective date: November 6, 2026

In short: this app collects no data about you and sends nothing anywhere. It has no ads, no analytics, no accounts, and no internet access.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information, usage data, device identifiers, location, contacts, or files.

Permissions

The app requests no Android permissions. Because it does not hold the INTERNET permission, it is not able to send information off your device.

What is stored on your device

The app saves two preferences in its own private storage so it remembers your last state:

  • the screen brightness you selected, and
  • whether the optional clock overlay is on.

These stay on your device. They are never transmitted, and they are removed when you uninstall the app. If you have Android's system backup enabled in your device settings, Android itself may include these preferences in your personal Google backup; that is handled by the operating system and your device settings, not by this app.

Display settings the app reads

To warn you when a color filter would tint the light, the app reads two read-only Android display settings on your device (night_display_activated and display_white_balance_activated). This is used on-screen only; the value is not stored, logged, or sent anywhere.

Third parties

There are none. The app contains no advertising networks, no analytics tools, and no third-party SDKs.

Sharing and selling

No data is shared, sold, or transferred, because no data is collected.

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Your choices

Uninstalling the app removes the preferences it stored on your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with an updated effective date.
